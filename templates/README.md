# FORGE Templates

Templates are project-facing artifacts generated or copied when FORGE initializes a product.

Planned templates include:

- project context
- decision records
- architecture records
- design briefs
- implementation plans
- verification reports
- release readiness reports

Templates should be intentionally small and should collect information that FORGE can actually use. Avoid creating forms that users must maintain manually unless the information is consequential and cannot be reliably inferred or generated.
